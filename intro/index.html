The problem

Why most new coins end badly

On most launchpads the price rises with every buy. Whoever buys first pays the least, and that is usually bots, insiders or the creator. When the price climbs, they sell to the people who arrived later.

Snipers buy first

Bots buy in the first seconds at the lowest price and sell into the first wave of real buyers.

The creator dumps

The creator can buy cheap at launch and sell everything at any moment, often minutes later.

There is no way back

If the coin never takes off, the buyers keep the loss. Nothing is returned.

How Sectora Launch works

A reserve first. A launch only when the goal is met.

01

Create

Anyone creates a coin for free: fixed supply, no owner, no mint, no tax. The creator picks the goal: $3,000, $7,000 or $15,000.

02

Reserve

Everyone pays the same price, up to $150, $350 or $500 per wallet, so every launch needs at least 20 wallets. You can take your money back at any time before launch.

03

Launch

At the goal the coin goes live on Uniswap, 50 % above the reserve price. The liquidity tokens are burned: nobody can ever withdraw them.

04

Delivery

75 % of your tokens at launch, the rest over 7 days. Lock for 30 days and earn a bonus of up to +25 %.

The goal is not reached in time? Everyone takes back 100 %. No fees.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can the creator run away with the money?

No. The reserve is held by the smart contract. At launch 98 % goes into the Uniswap pool and the LP tokens are burned. The creator only receives a 1 % fee and 5 % of the tokens over 30 days.

What happens if the reserve does not fill?

After the deadline everyone takes back 100 % of what they put in. You can also withdraw at any time before launch. No fees in either case.

Why is every coin priced in SECT?

SECT is the currency of the Sectora ecosystem. You can pay with ETH, USDT or SECT: ETH and USDT are swapped to SECT automatically on Uniswap.

Does this mean a coin cannot lose value?

No. The rules remove rug pulls and unfair advantages, but a new coin is still high risk: its price depends on the people who buy and sell it.

Which network does it use?

Ethereum today. SectoraEVM, Sectora's own network, is expected around March 2027.
